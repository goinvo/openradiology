Top Problems in Radiology

A ranked assessment of the structural failures facing diagnostic radiology, ranked by patient reach and monetary cost, and sourced from peer-reviewed journals, medical societies, and federal data.

Methodology

Each problem was evaluated on two independently scored dimensions, patient reach and monetary cost, drawn from peer-reviewed journals, federal datasets, and medical society reports. Scores were penalized when figures were indirect or not directly attributable to the problem. The combined total out of 100 determines the ranking.

Monetary Cost (M)

  • >$10B direct annual cost
  • $1B–$10B direct annual cost
  • $400M–$1B
  • $200M–$400M
  • <$200M or indirect figure

Patient Reach (P)

  • >100M directly affected
  • 30M–100M directly affected
  • 10M–30M or clear "1 in X" ratio
  • <10M or probabilistic/future harm
  • <5M or indirect impact
