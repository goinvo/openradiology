Key Statistics

01 · The workforce
3.6%

of all U.S. physicians are radiologists[1]

39,000 of 1,082,187 physicians[2]

0.305 interventional radiologists per 100,000 people, on average[3]

02 · The workload
870M

imaging scans performed in the U.S. yearly

Calculated by adding up the annual scan volumes for each imaging type above.

03 · The investment
$470B

out of $5.3 trillion U.S. yearly health spend is for radiology.[4][5]

Radiology accounts for nearly 8.9% of the spend. This figure excludes dental imaging.

04 · The reach
8 in 10

of all hospital visits in the U.S. involve some form of diagnostic imaging[6].

05 · The impact
+0.67yrs avg.

of life expectancy because of medical imaging[7].

06 · A typical day

In 1 day, a radiologist can read[8]:

35 CTs or MRI scans
or
100 mammograms
or
175 x-rays
07 · Accuracy
1 in 25

imaging scans have missed findings[9]

08 · The innovation
76% of all FDA-cleared, AI-enabled medical devices are for radiology[10]
